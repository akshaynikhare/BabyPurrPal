# BabyPurrPal audio licences

- **SFX, loops and music** (`sfx/*.ogg` and `music/*.ogg`, except the project-made files below): generated offline on 2026-11-01 with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) Sound Effects v3 (`eleven_text_to_sound_v3`; one-shots, loops, ambience, UI sounds) and Eleven Music v2.5 (`music_v2_5`, instrumental) on a paid Starter subscription, by `tools/audio/gen_sounds_eleven.py` from the prompts in `tools/audio/eleven_sounds.csv`. ElevenLabs allows commercial use of audio generated while on a paid plan ([help article](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361)); regenerate on a paid plan too. No third-party samples or recordings.
  - `chime_0` ... `chime_9` are one kalimba render (`sfx/kalimba.ogg`) retuned to the ten scale notes; `purr_tail` is cut from the `purr_loop` render. Both are made by the same script.
- **Project-made synth:** `sfx/babble.ogg` (from `tools/audio/render_sfx.py`, numpy/scipy, written for this project) and the smoke test tones below. Owned by the project.
- **Voice lines** (`voice/<pet>/*.wav`, `voice/<pet>/envelopes.json`): generated offline on 2026-11-01 with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) text to speech (Eleven v3 and v4) on a paid Starter subscription. ElevenLabs allows commercial use of audio generated while on a paid plan ([help article](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361)); regenerate on a paid plan too. Each pet's voice is an original synthetic voice made with ElevenLabs Voice Design from a text description (`tools/audio/design_voices.py`), not a clone of any real person. Lines are in `tools/audio/eleven_lines.csv`; `tools/audio/gen_voice_eleven.py` renders, picks and post-processes them and computes the mouth envelopes. The API key stays in `~/.elevenlabs/api_key`, outside the repo.
- **Talk-back**: the pet otherwise repeats the player's own recorded voice, pitch-shifted at runtime on the device; nothing is stored or shipped.
- `sfx/smoke_tone.ogg` and `music/smoke_loop.ogg` are internal test tones from the build pipeline.
