# BabyPurrPal audio licences

- **SFX and music** (`sfx/*.ogg`, `music/lullaby.ogg`): original renders from our own recipes in `tools/audio/render_sfx.py` (numpy/scipy synthesis, written for this project). No third-party samples or recordings. Owned by the project.
- **Voice**: no pre-rendered speech in this app. The pet repeats the player's own recorded voice, pitch-shifted at runtime.
- `smoke_tone.ogg` and `smoke_loop.ogg` are internal test tones from the build pipeline.
