# BabyPurrPal audio manifest

Generated by `tools/audio/make_manifest.py`. Load by name through `AudioDirector` (`assets/audio/{sfx,voice,music}/<name>.ogg`). All clips are mono 44.1 kHz OGG Vorbis.
SFX peak about -3 dBFS; loops (`purr_loop`, `purr_tail`) and `lullaby` peak about -6 dBFS.
The toy-system one-shots (`bounce_*`, `gulp_*`, `jingle_*`, `lap_*`, `munch_*`, `pop_in`, `pop_out`, `roll`, `slurp`, `splash`, `yum`) are levelled to about -19 LUFS (K-weighted, whole clip) like `crunch`, `giggle` and `paw_tap`, so their peaks sit between -2 and -11 dBFS.
There are no pre-rendered voice clips: spoken output is the player's own recorded voice, pitch-shifted at runtime (the `babble` clip is the mic-less fallback).
