# BabyPurrPal audio manifest

Generated by `tools/audio/make_manifest.py`. Load by name through `AudioDirector` (`assets/audio/{sfx,music}/<name>.ogg`).
SFX, loops and music are ElevenLabs renders from `tools/audio/eleven_sounds.csv` (`gen_sounds_eleven.py`): mono 44.1 kHz OGG Vorbis, trimmed and faded one-shots, seamless loops (loop=true in their .import files). A sound kept several times is `<id>_1` ... `<id>_N`; `AudioDirector.play_sfx(&"<id>")` plays one of them without an immediate repeat.
Levels (integrated LUFS, peaks soft-limited to -1 dBFS): one-shots -22, `ui_*` -25, loops -24, `amb_*` -30, music -20; pet voice lines -19, the reference. At runtime the SFX bus sits at -3 dB, music at -8 dB and ambience at -9 dB (on Loops), which puts one-shots about 6 dB, music about 9 dB and ambience about 20 dB under the voice; Master and SFX each have a -1 dB limiter.
Talk-back repeats the player's own recorded voice, pitch-shifted at runtime (the `babble` clip is the mic-less fallback).
Voice lines are ElevenLabs renders in one designed voice per pet (`tools/audio/design_voices.py`; voice ids in `eleven_voices.json`). The lines are in `eleven_lines.csv`, rendered as three takes each and picked with `gen_voice_eleven.py`, then post-processed by `gen_voice.postprocess`: 16-bit mono 24 kHz WAV, about -19 LUFS, peak at most -2 dBFS, with 100 ms of trailing silence. `PetVoice` plays the current pet's folder through `TalkBack.play_clip` at its own pitch (straight to the Voice bus), with mouth sync from each folder's `envelopes.json` and the mic gate.
